# Business Registration — Initial

- All business forms (sole proprietorship, partnership, Sdn Bhd, Bhd, LLP, foreign
  company, co-operative) must register with SSM within 30 days of starting operations.
- Operating unregistered is a criminal offence: fines up to RM50,000 or imprisonment
  up to 2 years (sole proprietorship/partnership) — this is a hard legal requirement,
  not a soft administrative one.
- Sole proprietorship/partnership: register via ezBiz, processed in 1–3 working days.
- Sdn Bhd: register via MyCoID, processed in 5–7 working days typically (up to 2 weeks).
- Sdn Bhd statutory incorporation fee: RM1,000, plus RM50 name reservation fee.
- Sole proprietorships are restricted to Malaysian citizens/PRs aged 18+; foreigners
  wanting to operate a business in Malaysia generally need a Sdn Bhd structure instead.

# Sdn Bhd — Ongoing Structural Requirements

- At least one director who ordinarily resides in Malaysia, and at least one shareholder.
- A company secretary must be appointed — vacancy beyond 30 days can draw SSM penalties;
  this deadline is enforced in practice, not merely theoretical.
- All company revenue should flow through a registered corporate bank account, not a
  personal account — mixing the two creates downstream accounting/tax/audit complications
  that compound at tax-filing and audit time.

# Annual Return (AR) Filing

- Every Sdn Bhd/Bhd must file an annual return within 30 days of the anniversary of
  incorporation — NOT tied to financial year end. This is one of the most common
  compliance trip-ups: founders confuse the AR clock with their accounting year.
- Example: incorporated 20 Jul 2022 → annual return due by 19 Aug each year
  (anniversary date + 30 days).
- Dormant companies are NOT exempt — a Sdn Bhd with zero transactions in a year must
  still file its annual return until formally struck off or wound up.
- Foreign companies with a registered branch/place of business in Malaysia are also
  subject to annual return obligations.
- Non-compliance: fines up to RM50,000, with liability extending to company directors
  personally, not just the company entity.

# Sole Proprietorship / Partnership Renewal

- Renewal can be done any time before expiry, up to 12 months after expiry, via SSM
  counter, EzBiz Kiosk, participating banks, or online (ezbiz.ssm.com.my).
- Each renewal period covers 1–5 years (business owner's choice within that range).
- Annual renewal fee: RM30 (personal name) or RM60 (trade name) for sole proprietorships.
- Non-renewal risks business closure and compounding penalties — distinct from the
  Sdn Bhd annual-return mechanism above; sole props and Sdn Bhd follow different
  compliance tracks entirely and should not be conflated.

# Financial Statements

- Sdn Bhd/Bhd companies must prepare and file audited financial statements annually,
  separate from (but feeding into) the annual return and Form C tax filing.
