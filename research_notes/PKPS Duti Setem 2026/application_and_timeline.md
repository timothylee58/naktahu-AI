# PKPS Duti Setem 2026: Application Process, Timeline, Market Impact (as of 2026-11-27)

Scope note: "PKPS" = Program Khas Pengakuan Sukarela (Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme, SVDP) Duti Setem 2026, run by LHDN/HASiL. It is a PENALTY WAIVER for late/unstamped instruments, NOT an exemption from stamp duty itself and NOT a property-buyer duty relief. Duty is still payable. Do not confuse with the first-time homebuyer stamp duty exemption (Budget 2026/2027), which is a separate measure. (Fetching hasil.gov.my directly was blocked in-session; official text below comes from search-engine extracts of the official PDFs via Exa.)

## 1. How to claim: steps, forms, portals, documents, who submits

### Takeaway
No separate PKPS application or penalty appeal exists. You submit the unstamped instrument through e-Duti Setem (inside MyTax) with a Borang Nyata Duti Setem (BNDS) and pay the duty within the programme window; the penalty is waived automatically. Manual filing is not allowed.

### Cited Findings
- Applications are made only by submitting the instrument online via the e-Duti Setem system; manual applications are not accepted — [HASiL FAQ (BM, updated 28 Jan 2026)](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf); [HASiL FAQ (EN)](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_Penalty_Exemption_Under_The_Special_Voluntary_Disclosure_Programme_PKPS_For_Stamp_Duty_2026.pdf)
- No penalty appeal needed; penalty waived automatically on payment. The BNDS/Notice of Assessment may still display a penalty, but it is shown under "Penalty Waived" and excluded from "Total Amount Payable" — [HASiL FAQ (EN)](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_Penalty_Exemption_Under_The_Special_Voluntary_Disclosure_Programme_PKPS_For_Stamp_Duty_2026.pdf); [HASiL article, Nanyang Siang Pau 7 Oct 2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261014-Nanyang-Siang-Pau-An-Opportunity-To-Settle-Outstanding-Stamp-Duty-Without-Penalties.pdf)
- Portal path: MyTax (https://mytax.hasil.gov.my) > Perkhidmatan ezHasil > Duti Setem > e-Duti Setem; the old STAMPS system was retired 31 Dec 2025 — [HASiL e-Buletin Edisi 2 2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/e-Buletin-HASiL-Edisi-2_2026.pdf); [HASiL media statement 17 Dec 2025](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251217-kenyataan-media-hasil-sistem-e-duti-setem-boleh-akses-lebih-awal-melalui-mytax-mulai-17-dis-2025.pdf)
- Steps (official STSDS procedure): log in to MyTax (TIN needed; register via e-Daftar if none) > e-Duti Setem, choose role (individual/agent) > select BNDS type and fill details, attach instrument > duty computed in real time > submit > pay within the stipulated period > stamp duty certificate generated after HASiL confirms payment > keep instrument and records 7 years — [HASiL media statement 17 Dec 2025](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251217-kenyataan-media-hasil-sistem-e-duti-setem-boleh-akses-lebih-awal-melalui-mytax-mulai-17-dis-2025.pdf)
- Phase 1 STSDS (since 1 Jan 2026) covers tenancy/lease, securities (e.g. loan/personal agreements) and general instruments; BNDS submission is deemed assessment on the submission date; payment due within 30 days of that date (s.36(2)), deadline shown on the acknowledgement slip. Property transfers (without JPPH valuation) move to self-assessment 1 Jan 2027; other instruments 1 Jan 2028; until then they keep formal assessment with a Notice of Assessment — [HASiL operational guideline 26 Dec 2025](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251226-garis-panduan-operasi-permohonan-penyeteman-melalui-sistem-taksir-sendiri-duti-setem.pdf)
- Supporting documents: the instrument itself plus related documents; examples given by HASiL: property transfer = Form KTN14A/ SPA, title/grant; securities = loan agreement + offer letter. TINs required for parties — [HASiL operational guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251226-garis-panduan-operasi-permohonan-penyeteman-melalui-sistem-taksir-sendiri-duti-setem.pdf)
- Who submits: duty payer or appointed agent; HASiL says eligible participants include companies, law firms, company secretaries, agencies and non-citizens — [HASiL article](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261014-Nanyang-Siang-Pau-An-Opportunity-To-Settle-Outstanding-Stamp-Duty-Without-Penalties.pdf); non-citizens eligible per [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_Penalty_Exemption_Under_The_Special_Voluntary_Disclosure_Programme_PKPS_For_Stamp_Duty_2026.pdf)
- Eligible: all instruments executed 1 Jan 2023 – 31 Dec 2025 not yet stamped/paid. If stamped (submitted) before 1 Jan 2026 but duty/penalty unpaid, still eligible if paid in the window. If duty AND penalty were fully paid before 1 Jan 2026: not eligible, no refund — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/FAQ_Penalty_Exemption_Under_The_Special_Voluntary_Disclosure_Programme_PKPS_For_Stamp_Duty_2026.pdf); [HASiL article](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261014-Nanyang-Siang-Pau-An-Opportunity-To-Settle-Outstanding-Stamp-Duty-Without-Penalties.pdf)
- Exclusions: fraud (deception/falsification with intent to evade); instruments executed in 2026 are outside the programme (HASiL article above). Instruments executed before 2023 are not covered either; DGIR said verbally (at a CTIM dialogue, reported by a law firm) that audits generally cover up to 3 years though the Act has a 5-year time bar — [Zic Legal](https://www.ziclegal.com/resources/regularising-the-past-the-2026-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-program) (commentary; verbal, non-binding)
- Audit protection: instruments stamped under PKPS will not be audited, but other unstamped instruments (and any stamped from 1 Jul 2026 outside PKPS eligibility) remain auditable — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf); [Tratax copy of guideline](https://tratax.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SDK-28-Jan-2026.pdf)
- Legal basis: s.47A(2) Stamp Act 1949 (Collector's remission power); operational guideline issued under s.76B, updated 29 Jun 2026 — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Related concession: no s.72D penalty for errors in BNDS submitted 1 Jan–31 Dec 2026 under STSDS (applies only to that limited penalty) — [Malaysian Bar Circular 466/2025](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20466-2025.pdf)
- Amendments: BNDS corrections via "permohonan pindaan"; amendment is not for seeking remission/relief under s.15/15A. Time-extension appeals for payment only for unpaid notices, filed before the due date — [HASiL operational guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251226-garis-panduan-operasi-permohonan-penyeteman-melalui-sistem-taksir-sendiri-duti-setem.pdf)
- Helpline: HCC 03-8911 1000, HASiL Live Chat, feedback form — [HASiL media statement 26 Jun 2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because the deadline requires payment (not just submission) by 31 Dec 2026, and formal-assessment instruments (e.g. property transfers) need a Notice of Assessment first, late-December submissions are risky. HASiL, EY and Deloitte all say so.

### Gaps
- Exhaustive per-instrument document checklist not found in accessible sources (HASiL direct fetch blocked). Whether the penalty-waived display behaves identically for every instrument type is untested.

## 2. Dates, extensions, penalties

### Takeaway
PKPS ran 1 Jan–30 Jun 2026, extended on 26 Jun 2026 by six months to 31 Dec 2026. Both stamping and payment must be completed by 31 Dec 2026. No further extension had been announced as at late Nov 2026; LHDN said the programme ends at year-end.

### Cited Findings
- Announced by the PM on 5 Jan 2026 (New Year address): penalty remission 1 Jan–30 Jun 2026 — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Extension to 31 Dec 2026 announced 26 Jun 2026 (Ministry of Finance approval, under a week before expiry) — [HASiL press release](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/06/malaysia-stamp-duty-extension-of-special-voluntary-disclosure-programme); [The Star 27 Jun 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/06/27/lhdn-extends-voluntary-stamp-duty-disclosure-to-dec-31)
- Payment after 31 Dec 2026 loses the waiver and attracts penalty even if submitted earlier — [EY alert](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-my/technical/tax-alerts/documents/ey-stamp-duty-special-voluntary-disclosure-program-06-26.pdf); [HASiL article](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20261014-Nanyang-Siang-Pau-An-Opportunity-To-Settle-Outstanding-Stamp-Duty-Without-Penalties.pdf)
- LHDN CEO on 31 Oct 2026: stamp duty programme ends at end of this year — [Malay Mail 31 Oct 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/31/stamp-duty-and-e-invoice-amnesty-windows-are-closing-irb-reminds-taxpayers/241417); on ~23 Nov 2026 reiterated "extended until December 31" — [Malay Mail 24 Nov 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/11/24/irb-govt-completes-rm2027b-in-tax-refunds-involving-365-million-cases/244494)
- Ordinary late-stamping penalties (s.47A): stamping within 3 months after the deadline = greater of RM50 or 10% of duty; otherwise greater of RM100 or 20%. Stamping deadline is 30 days from execution (s.47); duty payable within 30 days of deemed assessment (s.36(2)) — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf); [Zic Legal](https://www.ziclegal.com/resources/regularising-the-past-the-2026-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-program)
- Paying after window ends: late-payment penalty applies; not joining means penalties plus normal audit under the Stamp Duty Audit Framework — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf) (FAQ dates updated to 31 Dec in [MPMA circular 94/2026](https://mpma.org.my/upload/2026/Circular_EXTENSION_OF_THE_SPECIAL_VOLUNTARY_DISCLOSURE_PROGRAMME_FOR_STAMP_DUTY.pdf))
- Outside PKPS, Baker McKenzie notes a discretionary general voluntary disclosure route under the Stamp Duty Audit Framework can reduce penalty to 10%, only when no audit is underway — [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/06/malaysia-stamp-duty-extension-of-special-voluntary-disclosure-programme)
- Unstamped documents generally cannot be admitted as evidence in court until duty/penalty paid (s.52 context) — [Zic Legal](https://www.ziclegal.com/resources/regularising-the-past-the-2026-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-program); [August Advisory](https://www.augustadvisory.com.my/blog/182)
- Objection rights if you dispute an assessment: written notice to Collector within 30 days; appeal to High Court within 21 days of decision — [Zic Legal](https://www.ziclegal.com/resources/regularising-the-past-the-2026-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-program)
- STSDS Phase 2 (property transfers without JPPH valuation) starts 1 Jan 2027; Phase 3 on 1 Jan 2028 — [HASiL media statement](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20251217-kenyataan-media-hasil-sistem-e-duti-setem-boleh-akses-lebih-awal-melalui-mytax-mulai-17-dis-2025.pdf); [Malay Mail 24 Nov 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/11/24/irb-govt-completes-rm2027b-in-tax-refunds-involving-365-million-cases/244494)
- Inaccurate-BNDS penalty exemption stated to run throughout 2026 — [Malay Mail 24 Nov 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/11/24/irb-govt-completes-rm2027b-in-tax-refunds-involving-365-million-cases/244494)

### Inferences
- The June extension came days before expiry, so a second extension is conceivable, but no source says so; treat 31 Dec 2026 as final. LHDN's repeated "ends this year" messaging argues against one.

### Gaps
- No announcement of any further extension found up to 27 Nov 2026 (absence of evidence, not confirmation). Exact Notice-of-Assessment processing times for the year-end rush not found.

## 3. Reactions, impact, pitfalls

### Takeaway
Uptake has been large (over 1 million applications, ~RM184m penalties waived by 31 Oct per LHDN). Advisers (Big 4, Baker McKenzie, BDO) stress acting early because payment, not submission, must land by 31 Dec. I found no PKPS-specific statements from REHDA, HBA or MBA; the REHDA/Bar items found concern other stamp duty topics.

### Cited Findings
- Uptake: LHDN CEO said "nearly one million" applications on 1 Oct 2026 — [The Star 1 Oct 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/01/revenue-board-receives-nearly-1-million-applications-for-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure); more than 1.05 million applications as at 31 Oct, RM184m penalties fully remitted — [Malay Mail 24 Nov 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/11/24/irb-govt-completes-rm2027b-in-tax-refunds-involving-365-million-cases/244494). A consultancy blog reports 1m+ documents, RM471m duty stamped as at 30 Sep, attributed to the CEO on 31 Oct — [August Advisory](https://www.augustadvisory.com.my/blog/182) (secondary; the application count versus document count may differ)
- LHDN urged people to bring forward tenancy, loan and other transaction documents, noting its role is shifting to checks/audits under self-assessment — [The Star 1 Oct 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/01/revenue-board-receives-nearly-1-million-applications-for-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure)
- Practitioner advice: review 2023–2025 instruments (intra-group, commercial, financing, service agreements); submit early; payment by 31 Dec is the test — [BDO](https://www.bdo.my/en-gb/insights/tax/extension-of-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-programme); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/06/malaysia-stamp-duty-extension-of-special-voluntary-disclosure-programme); [Deloitte](https://www.taxathand.com/article/41498/Malaysia/2026/Update-on-Stamp-Duty-Special-Voluntary-Disclosure-Programme-2026); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_my/technical/tax-alerts/stamp-duty-special-voluntary-disclosure-program)
- Common pitfall (commentary): pre-2023 instruments are not covered yet remain legally dutiable; audit practice of ~3 years is administrative only — [Zic Legal](https://www.ziclegal.com/resources/regularising-the-past-the-2026-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-program)
- Lawyers' system complaints (Jan 2026, conveyancing; mainly RPGT/CKHT but also STAMPS/e-Duti Setem): system hangs, "Request failed" errors, unreachable helpline, slow processing, need to use personal lawyer MyTax logins pending a Firm Management system; Bar Council expressly adopted no position on LHDN replies — [Malaysian Bar Circular 011/2026](https://www.malaysianbar.org.my/cms/upload_files/document/Circular%20No%20011-2026.pdf)
- KPMG (16 Nov 2026): self-assessment shifts responsibility to taxpayers; ambiguity over who files/pays (incl. foreign parties), volume risk (e.g. purchase orders), audit readiness needed — [KPMG Budget 2027 article](https://kpmg.com/my/en/insights/2026/11/budget-2027--stamp-duty-reform---are-we-ready-for-the-next-phase.html) (general STSDS commentary, not PKPS-specific)
- REHDA and Malaysian Bar met 10 Nov 2026: topics were first-time-buyer stamp duty exemption for SOFO/SOHO, master-title fees, sub-sale direct transfers; PKPS not mentioned in the post — [REHDA LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rehdamalaysia_rehda-malaysia-hosted-an-engagement-session-activity-7526117942539800576-coZF)
- Separate measure (not PKPS): first-time homebuyer duty exemption (loan + transfer, up to RM500,000) extended in Budget 2026 to 31 Dec 2027 per search summary — [The Star Oct 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2025/10/10/budget-2026-stamp-duty-exemption-extended-for-first-time-homebuyers); Budget 2027 proposals reported in [The Star 9 Oct 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/09/budget-2027-stamp-duty-exemption-for-first-time-homebuyers) (not fetched; verify)

### Inferences
- PKPS mainly benefits tenancies, loan/financing documents and commercial/intra-group contracts (Phase 1 categories); property SPAs/transfers are less central, since property transfer self-assessment starts only in 2027. Developers/banks probably benefit mostly through legacy facility/loan documents, but no source confirms this.

Searches of The Edge, NST, Bernama returned nothing PKPS-specific beyond what is cited (Bernama/Berita Harian hits seen in search but not fetched). Impact on property market volumes: no data found.
- Bar Council's direct view on PKPS not found.
