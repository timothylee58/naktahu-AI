# PKPS Duti Setem 2026: scope and policy basis

Method note: only WebSearch worked. WebFetch failed (DNS block) for www.hasil.gov.my and www.bdo.my, so every finding below comes from search-result summaries, not from reading the full primary documents. Nothing was verified against the PDFs themselves.

## What does PKPS stand for and what is its purpose?

### Takeaway
In the stamp duty context PKPS is "Program Khas Pengakuan Sukarela (PKPS) Duti Setem 2026", the Stamp Duty Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme. It is a penalty waiver for late stamping, not an exemption from the duty itself. It is run by LHDN (HASiL, the Inland Revenue Board).

### Cited Findings
- PKPS = Program Khas Pengakuan Sukarela Duti Setem 2026 (stamp duty special voluntary disclosure programme). Instruments executed 1 Jan 2023 to 31 Dec 2025 qualify for penalty exemption. — [HASiL media statement 26 Jun 2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/media/vw0a0bvw/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf) (via search summary)
- Penalties are waived automatically once duty is paid; no penalty appeal is needed. — same HASiL statement (via search summary)
- Instruments regularised under PKPS will not be subject to stamp duty audit, but other unstamped instruments can still be audited. Fraud cases are excluded. — same HASiL statement (via search summary)
- A law firm lists these exclusions: fraud or forgery intended to avoid duty; duty and penalty fully paid before 1 Jan 2026; instruments submitted after the programme period. It says the programme is open to all duty payers, including non-citizens. — [DNH](https://dnh.com.my/missed-your-stamping-deadline-heres-how-to-get-a-late-stamping-penalty-waiver-under-pkps-2026/) (via search summary)
- The administering body is LHDN/HASiL. The operating guideline is titled "Garis Panduan Operasi Layanan Khas Penalti di bawah Seksyen 47A Akta Setem 1949 bagi PKPS Duti Setem 2026", which indicates the legal basis is s.47A of the Stamp Act 1949 (penalty remission). — [HASiL guideline PDF](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf) (title only; content not read)

### Inferences
- Other acronyms "PKPS" could stand for were not found in search results. Only the stamp duty programme appeared, but I did not run a dedicated disambiguation search.
- Because the relief is a s.47A penalty remission handled by operational guideline, there is probably no Stamp Duty (Exemption) Order for it. This is unconfirmed.

## Which instrument introduced it, and what are the order numbers and dates?

### Takeaway
Announced by the Prime Minister on 5 January 2026 (per one law firm), originally running 1 Jan to 30 Jun 2026, and extended by HASiL on 26 Jun 2026 to 31 Dec 2026. No gazetted Order number was found.

### Cited Findings
- The PM announced the programme on 5 January 2026, with full waiver of late-stamping penalties. — [DNH](https://dnh.com.my/missed-your-stamping-deadline-heres-how-to-get-a-late-stamping-penalty-waiver-under-pkps-2026/) (via search summary)
- Original period 1 Jan to 30 Jun 2026; extended six months from 1 Jul to 31 Dec 2026. Stamping and payment must fall within the programme period. — [HASiL statement 26 Jun 2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/media/vw0a0bvw/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf); also reported by [Bernama](https://bernama.com/bm/news.php?id=2573386) and [Berita Harian](https://www.bharian.com.my/amp/berita/nasional/2026/06/1578345/bhplus) (via search summaries)
- Also see [BDO: Extension of Stamp Duty Voluntary Disclosure Programme](https://www.bdo.my/en-gb/insights/tax/extension-of-stamp-duty-voluntary-disclosure-programme) (not read).

### Inferences
- A link to the Budget 2026 speech was not established. The PM announcement date (5 Jan 2026) suggests a separate announcement.
- It is new for 2026 and was amended once (extended) in June 2026. No predecessor PKPS was identified in these results.
