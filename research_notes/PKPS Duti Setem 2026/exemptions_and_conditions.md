# PKPS Duti Setem 2026 — exemptions, conditions, interaction with ad valorem duty

## Q1. What does "PKPS" mean here, and which instruments, property types, price bands, exemption rate and cap does it cover?

### Takeaway
PKPS = Program Khas Pengakuan Sukarela (Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme) Duti Setem 2026. It is a PENALTY remission (s.47A(2) Stamp Act 1949), NOT an exemption of the duty itself: no RM threshold, property price band, % duty exemption or cap exists. The duty is still payable in full; only late-stamping/late-payment penalties on instruments executed 1 Jan 2023 – 31 Dec 2025 are waived. (Duty-reducing reliefs, e.g. the first-home RM500k exemption, are a separate regime — see Q3.)

### Cited Findings
- Interpretation: I treated "PKPS" as the stamp-duty PKPS because it is the only PKPS in LHDN/HASiL stamp duty material; the user's framing ("exemption amounts/caps/price bands") does not match its actual nature. — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Announced by PM/Finance Minister on 5 Jan 2026 (New Year address) as penalty remission for 1 Jan–30 Jun 2026; extended by Ministry of Finance to 31 Dec 2026. — [HASiL guideline (updated 29 Jun 2026)](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Extension announced 26 Jun 2026: six months, 1 Jul–31 Dec 2026, citing adjustment to the Sistem Taksir Sendiri Duti Setem (STSDS, self-assessment). — [HASiL media statement](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf); [Bernama 26 Jun 2026](https://bernama.com/bm/news.php?id=2573386)
- Covered instruments: "all types" of instruments (agreements/documents signed) executed 1 Jan 2023–31 Dec 2025 and not yet stamped or duty not yet paid; so SPAs, loan agreements, transfers, tenancy etc. all qualify if in the date window. — [HASiL FAQ (28 Jan 2026)](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf); [Donovan & Ho](https://dnh.com.my/missed-your-stamping-deadline-heres-how-to-get-a-late-stamping-penalty-waiver-under-pkps-2026/)
- What is waived: s.47A penalties — RM50 or 10% of deficient duty (higher) if stamped within 3 months after the deadline; RM100 or 20% (higher) otherwise. Waiver is automatic on payment; no appeal needed. — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Normal stamping deadline: 30 days from execution in Malaysia (or from first receipt in Malaysia if signed abroad). — [Donovan & Ho](https://dnh.com.my/missed-your-stamping-deadline-heres-how-to-get-a-late-stamping-penalty-waiver-under-pkps-2026/)
- Applicable to all duty payers including non-citizens. — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf)
- Worked examples (from HASiL Lampiran A): instrument 1/3/2025, duty RM1,000, penalty RM200 (20%) shown on notice; paid 15/7/2026 → payable RM1,000 (penalty RM0). Instrument 1/12/2025, duty RM80,000, penalty RM16,000; paid 31/12/2026 → RM80,000; paid 2/1/2027 → RM96,000 (no waiver). Instrument dated 1/4/2022, duty RM8,000 → penalty RM1,600 still payable (outside window). Instrument dated 12/1/2026, duty RM1,000, paid 5/3/2026 → RM1,100 (RM100 late-payment penalty; 2026 instruments not eligible). — [HASiL guideline Lampiran A](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Penalty saving is up to 20% of duty (min RM100) per instrument; e.g. RM500,000 SPA at 3% tier-blended duty would save the 20% penalty only if overdue >3 months.
- No property-price cap, citizenship, first-home or developer condition applies to PKPS.

### Gaps
- Official PKPS guidance contains no RM caps/price bands because it is not a duty exemption; none sought further.

## Q2. Eligibility conditions (citizenship, completion deadlines, exclusions, clawback)?

### Takeaway
Eligibility is date-based: instrument executed 1 Jan 2023–31 Dec 2025, stamped AND duty paid within 1 Jan–31 Dec 2026, no fraud. Late final payment after 31 Dec 2026 forfeits the waiver. No clawback mechanism was found, but instruments stamped under PKPS are exempt from audit.

### Cited Findings
- Window: submit via e-Duti Setem (MyTax) online only; stamping and payment both between 1 Jan and 31 Dec 2026; final payment on/before 31 Dec 2026. — [HASiL guideline](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf); [FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf)
- Already stamped before 1 Jan 2026 but duty/penalty unpaid: still eligible if paid within window. Already paid duty plus penalty before 1 Jan 2026: NOT eligible (no refund). — [HASiL guideline para 4.5–4.6](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Exclusions: fraud/forgery with intent to evade duty; instruments dated before 1 Jan 2023 or from 1 Jan 2026; instruments paid after the programme period. — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf); [Donovan & Ho](https://dnh.com.my/missed-your-stamping-deadline-heres-how-to-get-a-late-stamping-penalty-waiver-under-pkps-2026/)
- Audit: instruments stamped under PKPS will not be audited, but other unstamped instruments (and any stamped from 1 Jan 2027) remain auditable. — [HASiL media statement](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/20260626-kenyataan-media-hasil-lanjutan-pkps-duti-setem-sehingga-31-dis-2026.pdf); [Bernama](https://bernama.com/bm/news.php?id=2573386)
- The penalty still displays on the BNDS/Notis Taksiran but is auto-written-off ("Penalti Dihapuskan") on payment. — [HASiL guideline para 4.10](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)
- Superseded: original period 1 Jan–30 Jun 2026 (FAQ of 28 Jan 2026 and Donovan & Ho still show 30 Jun); replaced by 31 Dec 2026 end-date. — [HASiL FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/soalan-lazim-faq-pengecualian-penalti-program-pengakuan-khas-sukarela-pkps-duti-setem-2026.pdf)
- Stamp duty administration moved entirely to e-Duti Setem on MyTax from 1 Jan 2026; phased STSDS 2026–2028 (2026 tenancy/lease, securities, general; 2027 property transfers without JPPH valuation; 2028 other). — [e-Buletin HASiL Edisi 2/2026](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/e-Buletin-HASiL-Edisi-2_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- A notice issued late in December (e.g. 28 Dec) leaves almost no payment time; submit early (HASiL itself advises this). Today (27 Nov 2026) leaves ~1 month.
- No developer/first-home/completion-deadline conditions apply to PKPS.

### Gaps
- No clawback/withdrawal provisions found beyond fraud exclusion; unverified whether waiver can be reversed later.

## Q3. How does it interact with ad valorem rates under the Stamp Act 1949 First Schedule, and what are the separate duty-reducing exemptions?

### Takeaway
PKPS is independent of the First Schedule: duty is computed at ordinary rates first, then only the s.47A penalty is removed. The genuine duty exemption in 2026 is the separate first-home relief (full exemption on transfer and loan instruments, homes up to RM500,000, to 31 Dec 2027), which can be combined with PKPS penalty waiver only if its own conditions are met (unverified).

### Cited Findings
- Budget 2026 (tabled 10 Oct 2025): full stamp duty exemption on transfer instruments and loan agreements for first-time buyers of homes priced up to RM500,000, extended two years to 31 Dec 2027; flat 4%–8% duty proposed on transfers of residential property to non-citizens and foreign companies (excluding PRs). — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2025/10/10/budget-2026-stamp-duty-exemption-extended-for-first-time-homebuyers)
- Exemption applied to Malaysian citizens (Bernama report as summarised by search; not directly read). — [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2619254)
- Budget 2027 (9 Oct 2026) PROPOSES: full exemption on first homes up to RM500,000, and homes up to RM750,000 with first RM500,000 exempt plus 50% exemption on the remainder, for SPAs executed 1 Jan 2027–31 Dec 2030. PROPOSAL only, not verified as gazetted. — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/10/09/budget-2027-govt-proposes-stamp-duty-exemptions-for-first-time-homebuyers); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/10/09/budget-2027-stamp-duty-exemption-proposed-for-first-homes-up-to-rm500000/238432)
- Worked example (PKPS only): duty RM3,000 on an SPA dated 15/1/2024, notice shows RM600 penalty; paid 7/9/2026 → pay RM3,000. — [HASiL guideline Lampiran A](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/garis-panduan-operasi-layanan-khas-penalti-di-bawah-seksyen-47a-akta-setem-1949-bagi-program-khas-pengakuan-sukarela-duti-setem-2026-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- Illustrative (my own arithmetic, standard memorandum-of-transfer rates 1%/2%/3%/4% on bands RM100k/RM400k/RM500k/above RM1m, from general knowledge, NOT verified here): RM400,000 transfer = 1,000 + 6,000 = RM7,000 duty; with first-home exemption RM0; late with PKPS = RM7,000 (penalty up to RM1,400 waived).
