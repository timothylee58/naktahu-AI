## Passport Renewal
Pembaharuan pasport Malaysia boleh dilakukan secara dalam talian melalui portal MyOnlinePassport di online.imi.gov.my atau di pejabat imigresen. Yuran pasport: Pasport 5 tahun (dewasa) RM200, Pasport 5 tahun (kanak-kanak di bawah 12 tahun) RM100, Pasport 2 tahun (kanak-kanak di bawah 5 tahun) RM100. Proses dalam talian memerlukan 3–5 hari bekerja dan penghantaran ke rumah tersedia.

## Passport Renewal
Malaysian passport renewal can be done online at online.imi.gov.my or at immigration offices. Fees: Adult 5-year passport RM200, Child 5-year (below 12) RM100, Child 2-year (below 5) RM100. Online processing takes 3–5 working days. Home delivery is available for an additional fee. Bring your old passport and MyKad for counter renewals.

## MyKad Replacement
Kad Pengenalan Penduduk (MyKad) boleh dipohon dan diganti di pejabat Jabatan Pendaftaran Negara (JPN). Penggantian MyKad hilang/rosak: RM10 (kali pertama), RM20 (kali kedua), RM40 (kali ketiga dan seterusnya). Rakyat Malaysia perlu membuat MyKad apabila mencapai umur 12 tahun. MyKad wajib dibawa pada setiap masa.

## MyKad Replacement
MyKad (National Registration Identity Card) applications and replacements are made at JPN (National Registration Department) offices. Replacement fees: RM10 (1st time), RM20 (2nd time), RM40 (3rd time and beyond). All Malaysians must obtain a MyKad at age 12. MyKad must be carried at all times. Digital MyKad (MyDigital ID) is being introduced.

## Malaysia Visa Types
Jenis visa Malaysia untuk pelawat asing: 1. Visa Pelawat (Visit Visa) — pelancong, melawat keluarga. 2. Visa Pelajar — pelajar pengajian di Malaysia. 3. Visa Pekerjaan — pekerja asing dengan permit kerja. 4. Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) — pemastautin jangka panjang. 5. Visa Transit — transit di Malaysia. Warganegara dari negara-negara ASEAN, kebanyakan negara komanwel, dan banyak negara lain dikecualikan dari visa pelawat.

## Malaysia Visa Types
Malaysia visa types for foreign visitors: 1. Visit Visa — tourists and family visits. 2. Student Pass — for students studying in Malaysia. 3. Employment Pass — for skilled foreign workers. 4. Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) — long-term residency. 5. Transit Visa. Citizens of ASEAN countries, most Commonwealth nations, and many others do not require a visa for short visits.

## Permanent Residency Application
Permohonan Pemastautin Tetap (PR) Malaysia: Pemohon asing boleh memohon PR setelah menetap secara sah di Malaysia selama sekurang-kurangnya 10 tahun berterusan. Syarat tambahan: tidak pernah disabitkan kesalahan jenayah, mampu bertutur Bahasa Malaysia, bermastautin secara tetap. Pasangan warganegara Malaysia boleh memohon selepas 5 tahun kahwin. Permohonan melalui Jabatan Imigresen.

## Permanent Residency Application
Foreign nationals can apply for Malaysia Permanent Residency (PR) after legally residing in Malaysia for at least 10 consecutive years. Additional requirements: no criminal record, able to speak Bahasa Malaysia, intention to reside permanently. Spouses of Malaysian citizens may apply after 5 years of marriage. Applications are submitted to the Immigration Department.

## MM2H Programme
Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) program membenarkan warganegara asing bermastautin jangka panjang di Malaysia. Syarat semasa (2024): baki akaun simpanan minimum RM500,000 (bekas: RM300,000), aset cair offshore minimum RM1.5 juta, simpanan tetap RM1 juta, pendapatan bulanan luar negara minimum RM40,000. Had umur minimum 35 tahun. Visa MM2H sah 5 tahun.

## MM2H Programme
Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) allows foreign nationals to reside long-term in Malaysia on a 5-year renewable visa. Current requirements (revised 2024): minimum savings account balance RM500,000, offshore liquid assets RM1.5 million, fixed deposit RM1 million, offshore monthly income minimum RM40,000. Minimum age 35. The programme is managed by the Ministry of Tourism.

## Employment Pass Malaysia
Permit Pekerjaan (Employment Pass) untuk pekerja asing berkemahiran tinggi di Malaysia: EP Kategori I (gaji RM10,000 ke atas) — sah sehingga 5 tahun. EP Kategori II (gaji RM5,000–RM9,999) — sah sehingga 3 tahun. EP Kategori III (gaji RM3,000–RM4,999, kontrak ≤2 tahun) — sah mengikut kontrak. Permohonan melalui Expatriate Services Division (ESD) atau Talent Corporation.

## Employment Pass Malaysia
Malaysia Employment Pass for skilled foreign workers: Category I (salary RM10,000+) — valid up to 5 years. Category II (salary RM5,000–RM9,999) — valid up to 3 years. Category III (salary RM3,000–RM4,999, contract ≤2 years) — valid per contract duration. Apply through Expatriate Services Division (ESD) at esd.imi.gov.my or via TalentCorp.

## Dependant Pass Malaysia
Ibu bapa asing yang mempunyai anak warganegara Malaysia boleh memohon Pas Lawatan (Izin Tinggal) melalui Jabatan Imigresen. Pas ini membenarkan ibu bapa tinggal di Malaysia bersama anak mereka. Perlu diperbaharui setiap tahun. Pemohon tidak dibenarkan bekerja dengan pas ini melainkan mendapat permit kerja berasingan.

## Dependant Pass Malaysia
Foreign parents of Malaysian citizen children may apply for a Visitor's Pass (Temporary Employment/Social Visit) through the Immigration Department. This pass allows parents to reside in Malaysia with their child and is renewed annually. The holder cannot work in Malaysia unless they obtain a separate Employment Pass.
