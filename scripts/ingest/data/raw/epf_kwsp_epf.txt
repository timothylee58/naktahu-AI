## EPF Contribution Rates
Kadar caruman KWSP bagi pekerja di bawah umur 60 tahun: Pekerja menyumbang 11% daripada gaji bulanan. Majikan menyumbang 13% bagi pekerja bergaji RM5,000 ke bawah, dan 12% bagi pekerja bergaji melebihi RM5,000. Bagi pekerja berumur 60 tahun ke atas: Pekerja 5.5%, Majikan 4%.

## EPF Contribution Rates
EPF (KWSP) contribution rates for employees below 60 years: Employee contributes 11% of monthly wages. Employer contributes 13% for employees earning RM5,000 and below, or 12% for employees earning above RM5,000. For employees aged 60 and above: Employee 5.5%, Employer 4%.

## EPF Withdrawal Types
Pengeluaran KWSP tersedia dalam pelbagai jenis: 1. Pengeluaran Umur 55 Tahun — pengeluaran penuh apabila mencapai umur 55 tahun. 2. Pengeluaran Akaun 2 bagi pembelian rumah. 3. Pengeluaran untuk pendidikan anak. 4. Pengeluaran kesihatan bagi penyakit kritikal. 5. Pengeluaran kematian — waris boleh menuntut baki. 6. Pengeluaran untuk incapacitation (hilang upaya). 7. Pengeluaran penghijrahan (meninggalkan Malaysia secara kekal).

## EPF Withdrawal Types
EPF (KWSP) withdrawal options include: 1. Age 55 Withdrawal — full withdrawal upon reaching 55. 2. Account 2 housing withdrawal for property purchase. 3. Education withdrawal for children's tertiary education. 4. Health withdrawal for critical illness treatment. 5. Death withdrawal — beneficiaries claim the balance. 6. Incapacitation withdrawal. 7. Leaving Malaysia permanently withdrawal. 8. Flexible Account (Akaun Fleksibel) withdrawals introduced in 2024.

## EPF New Account Structure 2024
Struktur Akaun KWSP baharu (2024): Akaun Persaraan (75%) — untuk persaraan. Akaun Sejahtera (15%) — untuk pendidikan, perumahan, kesihatan. Akaun Fleksibel (10%) — boleh dikeluarkan bila-bila masa tanpa syarat. Perubahan ini berkuat kuasa Mei 2024. Pengeluaran Akaun Fleksibel boleh dilakukan melalui i-Akaun atau kaunter KWSP.

## EPF New Account Structure 2024
EPF new three-account structure effective May 2024: Retirement Account (75%) — for retirement savings. Wellbeing Account (15%) — for education, housing, and health withdrawals. Flexible Account (10%) — can be withdrawn at any time without conditions. Withdrawals from the Flexible Account can be made via i-Akaun app or EPF counters.

## i-Akaun Digital Services
i-Akaun ialah platform digital KWSP untuk ahli mengurus akaun mereka. Melalui i-Akaun, ahli boleh: menyemak baki akaun, memohon pengeluaran, mengemas kini maklumat peribadi, menyemak penyata caruman, mendaftar i-Invest untuk melabur simpanan melebihi had am. Pendaftaran di i.kwsp.gov.my atau muat turun apl i-Akaun.

## i-Akaun Digital Services
i-Akaun is EPF's digital platform for members. Through i-Akaun, members can: check account balance, apply for withdrawals, update personal information, view contribution statements, and register for i-Invest to invest savings above the basic savings amount. Register at i.kwsp.gov.my or download the i-Akaun mobile app.

## EPF Dividend History
Dividen KWSP untuk tahun 2023: 5.50% bagi caruman konvensional (Simpanan Konvensional). 5.40% bagi Simpanan Shariah. Dividen KWSP dikreditkan terus ke dalam akaun ahli pada bulan Februari/Mac tahun berikutnya. KWSP mengisytiharkan dividen berasaskan prestasi pelaburan dana.

## EPF Dividend History
EPF dividend for 2023: 5.50% for Conventional Savings. 5.40% for Shariah Savings. EPF dividends are credited directly to member accounts in February/March of the following year. The dividend rate is based on EPF's investment returns.

## EPF Housing Withdrawal
Pengeluaran KWSP untuk perumahan (Akaun Sejahtera): Ahli boleh mengeluarkan simpanan untuk: membeli rumah kediaman pertama, membayar balik pinjaman perumahan, membiayai pembinaan rumah. Syarat: ahli mesti berumur bawah 55 tahun, tidak pernah membuat pengeluaran perumahan sebelum ini (bagi pembelian pertama). Had pengeluaran bergantung kepada harga rumah dan baki akaun.

## EPF Housing Withdrawal
EPF housing withdrawal (Wellbeing Account): Members can withdraw savings to: purchase a first residential property, repay a housing loan, or finance house construction. Conditions: member must be under 55 years old. Withdrawal limit depends on the property price and account balance. Application can be made online via i-Akaun or at EPF branches.

## EPF Education Withdrawal
Pengeluaran KWSP untuk pendidikan: Ahli atau anak-anak ahli yang belajar di institusi pengajian tinggi tempatan atau luar negara yang diiktiraf boleh mengeluarkan simpanan Akaun Sejahtera. Permohonan perlu diserahkan sebelum memulakan pengajian atau dalam tempoh semester pertama. Jumlah pengeluaran bergantung kepada yuran pengajian.

## EPF Education Withdrawal
EPF education withdrawal: Members or their children studying at recognized local or overseas higher education institutions can withdraw from the Wellbeing Account. Application must be submitted before studies begin or within the first semester. The amount is based on tuition fees. This withdrawal does not affect the member's basic savings requirement.

## EPF Contribution Rules
Caruman minimum KWSP: Bagi pekerja yang bergaji RM5,000 ke bawah, majikan wajib menyumbang 13%. Pekerja mandiri (self-employed) boleh mendaftar sebagai ahli KWSP secara sukarela dan memilih kadar caruman sendiri. Majikan perlu mengemukakan caruman setiap bulan sebelum atau pada 15 haribulan bulan berikutnya.
