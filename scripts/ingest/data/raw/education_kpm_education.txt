## SPM Examination Guide
Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM) ialah peperiksaan awam bagi pelajar Tingkatan 5. Mata pelajaran wajib SPM: Bahasa Malaysia (wajib lulus), Bahasa Inggeris, Sejarah (wajib lulus), Matematik, Pendidikan Islam/Pendidikan Moral. Pelajar perlu menduduki sekurang-kurangnya Bahasa Malaysia dan Sejarah. Peperiksaan SPM diadakan setiap November/Disember. Keputusan diumumkan Mac tahun berikutnya.

## SPM Examination Guide
SPM (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia) is the public examination for Form 5 students. Compulsory subjects: Bahasa Malaysia (must pass), English, History (must pass), Mathematics, and Islamic Education/Moral Education. Students must sit for Bahasa Malaysia and Sejarah at minimum. SPM is held every November/December, with results announced in March the following year.

## SPM Grading System
Sistem Gred SPM: Gred A+ (markah 90–100), A (80–89), A- (75–79), B+ (70–74), B (65–69), C+ (60–64), C (55–59), D (40–54), E (20–39), G (di bawah 20). Gred A, A+, dan A- dikira dalam pengiraan purata gred (GPA). Pelajar perlu lulus Bahasa Malaysia dan Sejarah untuk sijil SPM.

## SPM Grading System
SPM grading system: A+ (90–100), A (80–89), A- (75–79), B+ (70–74), B (65–69), C+ (60–64), C (55–59), D (40–54), E (20–39), G (below 20). Grades A, A+, and A- count towards GPA calculation. Students must pass Bahasa Malaysia and Sejarah (History) to receive the SPM certificate.

## STPM Examination
Sijil Tinggi Persekolahan Malaysia (STPM) adalah peperiksaan pra-universiti setara A-Level. STPM diajar dalam 3 semester (18 bulan). Penilaian terdiri daripada peperiksaan akhir semester dan kerja kursus (pentaksiran berasaskan sekolah). Gred STPM: A (markah 80–100), A- (70–79), B+ (65–69), B (60–64), B- (55–59), C+ (50–54), C (45–49), C- (40–44), D+ (35–39), D (30–34), F (di bawah 30).

## STPM Examination
STPM (Sijil Tinggi Persekolahan Malaysia) is a pre-university qualification equivalent to A-Levels. STPM spans 3 semesters (18 months). Assessment combines final semester exams and coursework. Grades range from A (80–100) to F (below 30). STPM is administered by the Malaysian Examinations Council (MPM/Majlis Peperiksaan Malaysia).

## JPA Scholarship
Biasiswa JPA (Jabatan Perkhidmatan Awam) ditawarkan kepada pelajar cemerlang untuk pengajian di luar negara dan tempatan. Jenis biasiswa: 1. Program Khas (luar negara) — 150 biasiswa setahun. 2. Program Separuh Biasiswa. Syarat umum: Warganegara Malaysia, tawaran universiti yang diluluskan, pencapaian akademik cemerlang (SPM/STPM). Permohonan dibuka pada bulan Februari–Mac setiap tahun.

## JPA Scholarship
JPA (Public Service Department) scholarships are offered to outstanding students for studies overseas and locally. Types: 1. Special Programme (overseas) — approximately 150 scholarships per year. 2. Half Scholarship programme. General criteria: Malaysian citizen, offer letter from approved university, excellent academic results. Applications open February–March each year at biasiswa.jpa.gov.my.

## PTPTN Education Loan
PTPTN (Perbadanan Tabung Pendidikan Tinggi Nasional) menyediakan pinjaman pendidikan kepada pelajar pengajian tinggi. Kadar pinjaman bergantung kepada institusi dan program: RM1,000–RM10,000 setiap semester. Pelajar perlu memulangkan pinjaman selepas tamat pengajian dan mendapat pekerjaan. Potongan automatik gaji (Auto Debit) tersedia. Pelajar dengan CGPA 3.00 ke atas mendapat rebat 20%.

## PTPTN Education Loan
PTPTN (National Higher Education Fund Corporation) provides education loans to tertiary students. Loan amounts depend on institution and programme type: RM1,000–RM10,000 per semester. Repayment begins after graduation and employment. Auto debit from salary is available. Students who achieve CGPA 3.00 and above receive a 20% rebate on the loan amount.

## MRSM Application
MRSM (Maktab Rendah Sains MARA) adalah sekolah berasrama penuh yang dikelola oleh MARA untuk pelajar bumiputera yang cemerlang. Terdapat lebih 50 MRSM di seluruh Malaysia. Kemasukan ke MRSM Tingkatan 1 dan 4 berdasarkan keputusan PT3 (Tingkatan 3) atau UPSR (Tingkatan 1). Permohonan dibuat melalui portal MARA di mara.gov.my.

## MRSM Application
MRSM (MARA Junior Science College) is a residential school managed by MARA for outstanding Bumiputera students. There are over 50 MRSMs nationwide. Admission to Form 1 is based on UPSR results; Form 4 admission is based on PT3 results. Applications are made through the MARA portal at mara.gov.my. Selection is competitive and merit-based.

## SBP Boarding School
Sekolah Berasrama Penuh (SBP) adalah sekolah menengah berasrama yang dikelola oleh Kementerian Pendidikan Malaysia (KPM). SBP menerima pelajar Tingkatan 1 dan Tingkatan 4 berdasarkan keputusan peperiksaan. Terdapat 37 SBP di seluruh Malaysia. Pelajar dari pelbagai kaum boleh memohon. Permohonan melalui portal KPM.

## SBP Boarding School
Sekolah Berasrama Penuh (SBP/Fully Residential Schools) are boarding secondary schools managed by the Ministry of Education Malaysia. SBPs accept Form 1 (based on UPSR) and Form 4 (based on PT3) students. There are 37 SBPs nationwide, open to students of all races. Applications are made through the MOE portal.

## PT3 Assessment
Penilaian Menengah Rendah (PT3) ialah penilaian bagi pelajar Tingkatan 3 yang dijalankan oleh Lembaga Peperiksaan Malaysia (LPM). PT3 dinilai melalui gabungan peperiksaan bertulis, pentaksiran sekolah, dan ujian lisan (Bahasa Malaysia/Inggeris). Keputusan PT3 digunakan untuk kemasukan ke SBP, MRSM, dan sekolah vokasional.

## Public University Admission
Universiti awam (UA) di Malaysia termasuk Universiti Malaya (UM), UKM, UPM, UTM, USM, UiTM (bumiputera), UPSI, UMS, UNIMAS, dan lain-lain. Kemasukan ke UA melalui UPU (Unit Pusat Universiti) berasaskan keputusan STPM, matrikulasi, atau diploma. Permohonan UPU dibuat di upu.mohe.gov.my biasanya pada bulan Mac–April.
