## Income Tax Rate Schedule YA2023
Cukai pendapatan individu di Malaysia dikenakan secara progresif. Bagi Tahun Taksiran 2023, kadar cukai adalah seperti berikut: Pendapatan bercukai RM0–RM5,000 dikenakan cukai 0%. RM5,001–RM20,000 dikenakan 1%. RM20,001–RM35,000 dikenakan 3%. RM35,001–RM50,000 dikenakan 6%. RM50,001–RM70,000 dikenakan 11%. RM70,001–RM100,000 dikenakan 19%. RM100,001–RM400,000 dikenakan 25%. RM400,001–RM600,000 dikenakan 26%. RM600,001–RM2,000,000 dikenakan 28%. Melebihi RM2,000,000 dikenakan 30%.

## Income Tax Rate Schedule YA2023
Malaysian individual income tax uses a progressive rate for Year of Assessment 2023: Taxable income RM0–RM5,000 is taxed at 0%. RM5,001–RM20,000 at 1%. RM20,001–RM35,000 at 3%. RM35,001–RM50,000 at 6%. RM50,001–RM70,000 at 11%. RM70,001–RM100,000 at 19%. RM100,001–RM400,000 at 25%. RM400,001–RM600,000 at 26%. RM600,001–RM2,000,000 at 28%. Above RM2,000,000 at 30%.

## e-Filing Deadlines
Tarikh akhir penghantaran borang cukai pendapatan e-Filing bagi individu (BE) adalah 30 April setiap tahun. Bagi individu yang mempunyai pendapatan perniagaan (Borang B), tarikh akhir ialah 30 Jun. Individu yang tidak menjalankan perniagaan menggunakan Borang BE. Denda RM200 hingga RM20,000 atau penjara tidak lebih 6 bulan boleh dikenakan jika gagal mengemukakan.

## e-Filing Deadlines
The deadline for individual income tax e-Filing (Form BE) is 30 April each year. For individuals with business income (Form B), the deadline is 30 June. Failure to submit on time can result in a fine of RM200 to RM20,000 or imprisonment not exceeding 6 months.

## Personal Tax Relief YA2023
Pelepasan cukai individu bagi Tahun Taksiran 2023 termasuk: Pelepasan diri RM9,000. Pasangan RM4,000. Anak di bawah 18 tahun RM2,000 setiap seorang. Anak dalam pengajian tinggi RM8,000. Pelepasan SSPN RM8,000. Insurans nyawa dan KWSP sehingga RM7,000. Rawatan perubatan ibu bapa RM8,000. Perbelanjaan perubatan diri/pasangan/anak RM10,000. Langganan sukan/rekreasi RM1,000. Pembelian buku/majalah/jurnal RM2,500.

## Personal Tax Relief YA2023
Individual tax reliefs for Year of Assessment 2023: Self relief RM9,000. Spouse relief RM4,000. Child below 18 years RM2,000 each. Child in higher education RM8,000. SSPN savings RM8,000. Life insurance and EPF contributions up to RM7,000. Parents' medical treatment RM8,000. Own/spouse/child medical expenses RM10,000. Sports/recreation subscriptions RM1,000. Books/magazines/journals RM2,500. Lifestyle relief (tech equipment, internet, sports) RM2,500.

## e-Filing Registration Guide
Pendaftaran e-Filing LHDN: 1. Layari ezHasil di mytax.hasil.gov.my. 2. Pilih 'e-Filing' dan klik 'Daftar ID Pengguna'. 3. Masukkan nombor MyKad dan maklumat peribadi. 4. PIN akan dikirimkan ke alamat berdaftar. 5. Log masuk menggunakan ID Pengguna dan PIN. 6. Tukar PIN kepada kata laluan peribadi. Individu perlu mendaftar sekali sahaja. Pengemukaan cukai hanya perlu dilakukan apabila pendapatan melebihi RM34,000 setahun.

## e-Filing Registration Guide
To register for LHDN e-Filing: 1. Visit mytax.hasil.gov.my (ezHasil portal). 2. Select 'e-Filing' then 'Register'. 3. Enter your MyKad number and personal details. 4. A PIN will be mailed to your registered address. 5. Log in with your User ID and PIN. 6. Change PIN to a personal password. You only need to register once. Filing is required if annual income exceeds RM34,000.

## Real Property Gains Tax (RPGT)
Cukai Keuntungan Harta Tanah (CKHT/RPGT) dikenakan ke atas keuntungan daripada pelupusan harta tanah. Bagi warganegara dan pemastautin tetap: pelupusan dalam 3 tahun pertama dikenakan 30%, tahun ke-4 dikenakan 20%, tahun ke-5 dikenakan 15%, tahun ke-6 dan seterusnya dikenakan 0%. Harta pusaka dikecualikan. Had pengecualian once-in-a-lifetime RM10,000 atau 10% daripada keuntungan.

Real Property Gains Tax (RPGT) in Malaysia applies to gains from disposal of real property. For citizens and permanent residents: disposal within 3 years = 30%, year 4 = 20%, year 5 = 15%, year 6 and beyond = 0%. Inherited property is exempt. Once-in-a-lifetime exemption of RM10,000 or 10% of the chargeable gain (whichever is higher) is available.

## Stamp Duty on Property Transfer
Duti Setem ke atas pindah milik hartanah: Harga pembelian RM100,000 pertama: 1%. RM100,001 hingga RM500,000: 2%. RM500,001 hingga RM1,000,000: 3%. Melebihi RM1,000,000: 4%. Pengecualian duti setem tersedia untuk pembeli rumah pertama bagi harta berharga tidak melebihi RM500,000 (dikecualikan sepenuhnya bagi harta RM300,000 ke bawah).

## Stamp Duty on Property Transfer
Stamp duty on property transfer: First RM100,000 = 1%. RM100,001 to RM500,000 = 2%. RM500,001 to RM1,000,000 = 3%. Above RM1,000,000 = 4%. First-time homebuyers are exempt from stamp duty on properties priced up to RM300,000. Partial exemption for properties between RM300,001 and RM500,000.

## Sales and Service Tax (SST)
Cukai jualan dan perkhidmatan (SST) di Malaysia terdiri daripada Cukai Jualan (5% atau 10%) ke atas barangan dan Cukai Perkhidmatan (8%) ke atas perkhidmatan bercukai. SST menggantikan GST sejak September 2018. Perniagaan perlu mendaftar SST jika jumlah jualan barangan bercukai melebihi RM500,000 setahun atau nilai perkhidmatan bercukai melebihi RM500,000 setahun.

Malaysia's Sales and Service Tax (SST): Sales Tax is 5% or 10% on taxable goods. Service Tax is 8% on taxable services. SST replaced GST in September 2018. Businesses must register for SST if taxable goods sales exceed RM500,000 per year, or taxable services value exceeds RM500,000 per year. Registration is done through the MyST portal at mysst.customs.gov.my.

## Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB/MTD)
Potongan Cukai Bulanan (PCB) atau Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD) ialah sistem di mana majikan memotong cukai anggota pekerja setiap bulan. Pekerja yang telah PCB tidak perlu membayar cukai tambahan. Untuk semak jumlah PCB, gunakan Kalkulator PCB di laman LHDN. Pekerja boleh mengemukakan borang TP3 kepada majikan untuk mengambilkira relief cukai.

## Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB/MTD)
Monthly Tax Deduction (MTD/PCB) is a system where employers deduct estimated income tax from employees' monthly salary. Employees whose only income is employment income and whose MTD is accurate may not need to file a tax return. Use LHDN's PCB Calculator on the ezHasil portal to estimate monthly deductions. Submit Form TP3 to your employer to factor in personal reliefs.

## Tax Return Forms
Borang cukai individu di Malaysia: Borang BE untuk pekerja yang tiada pendapatan perniagaan. Borang B untuk individu dengan pendapatan perniagaan. Borang M untuk bukan pemastautin. Borang P untuk perkongsian. Semua borang boleh dikemukakan melalui portal ezHasil di mytax.hasil.gov.my.
